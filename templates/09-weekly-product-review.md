One meeting, same day, same agenda, every product in development, every week.
Source: `references/13-the-weekly-product-review.md` [FORTUNE08 p9, BW04, MIT92]. The format is our suggestion,
drawn from his description of Apple's Monday meetings.

## Rules (our suggestion)

- **Every line, every week.** No change gets 60 seconds. Trouble gets a decision or a named owner and date.
- **Owners, not departments.** If the room is too big to argue in, it's too big. His examples were small
  groups: eight at the top of NeXT [MIT92], six in an ad hoc idea meeting [BW04].
- **Ideas go elsewhere.** New ideas go to an ad hoc session of a handful of people, called by whoever has the
  idea [BW04]. The result comes back as a line.
- **Process is for efficiency.** "Process makes you more efficient." [BW04] Don't use this meeting to generate
  products.
